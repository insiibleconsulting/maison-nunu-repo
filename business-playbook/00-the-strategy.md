# 00 — The strategy

Read this before doing anything else in this folder. It is short on purpose.

---

## Where the money actually comes from

There is no checkout. Every sale happens inside a WhatsApp conversation, in person, by appointment.
That is not a limitation to work around. It is the business model, and it changes what social media
is for.

The funnel, honestly:

```
  someone sees a piece          Instagram, TikTok, a friend's phone
          ↓
  they believe you are real     grid, replies, faces, repeat customers
          ↓
  they open WhatsApp            the only conversion that counts
          ↓
  you answer within the hour    this is the competitive advantage
          ↓
  you agree a price and a time  cash or mobile money, in person
```

Three of those five steps are conversation, not content. Most small shops pour everything into
step one and lose the sale at step four by replying nine hours later. Do not be that shop.

---

## What Maison Nunu is competing on

It resells. Other people made the pieces, and in most cases other shops can get them too. So the
defensible ground is not the product. It is:

**Taste.** A selection that looks chosen rather than listed. Eight collections, edited, in a
consistent visual world. This is what the website's restraint is for and what the grid has to
continue.

**Speed.** A reply inside an hour beats a reply inside a day, every time, and it costs nothing.

**Access.** "I can source that" is worth more than any post. The website already promises it
(`/shop/` says the site shows what we carry, not everything we can get). Confirm it is true, then
lean on it hard, because it is the one thing a bigger shop cannot copy quickly.

**Presence.** A person, with a name and a face, who has been posting consistently for six months.
In a market where anyone can open an Instagram shop and vanish, continuity is credibility.

## What to measure

Six numbers. Review them monthly, in `05-inventory-and-sales.md`'s Dashboard tab.

| Number | Why it is the one that matters | Healthy first-year signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Enquiries per week** | The only true top-of-funnel metric. A WhatsApp thread opened. | Growing month on month |
| **Enquiry → sale rate** | Tests your replies, your prices and your photos at once. | 15–30% |
| **Average order value** | Tells you whether to push stacking pieces or statement pieces. | Track it, then raise it |
| **Gross margin %** | Sell price minus cost price. The only number that pays you. | Know it per piece |
| **Days to sell** | How long cash sits in a piece. A reseller's real risk. | Flag anything over 90 |
| **Saves and shares** | Intent. A save is someone planning to buy. | Watch instead of likes |

### What to ignore

**Follower count.** A thousand followers who will never travel to Douala are worth less than eighty
who will. Do not buy followers, do not run follow-for-follow, and do not let the number set the mood
of the week.

**Likes.** They correlate with nothing here. Saves, shares and profile taps are the ones that move
before a DM arrives.

**Posting every day because someone said so.** Three good posts a week for a year beats daily for a
month and then silence. Silence is the thing that kills a small shop's credibility, and burnout is
what causes silence.

## The three risks worth naming

**1. Cash locked in stock that does not move.** This is how small retailers die, and it is invisible
until it is severe. A piece that has sat for 120 days is not inventory, it is a loan you made to
yourself. The Dashboard tab surfaces this deliberately. Discount it, bundle it, or use it as a gift
with purchase, but do not let it sit while you buy more.

**2. The one-channel problem.** If Instagram suspends the account, what remains? The answer has to
be: the WhatsApp contact list, the customer rows in the sales sheet, and the website on its own
domain. All three are owned. Build them from day one. Export the customer list monthly.

**3. Saying something the business cannot keep.** A delivery promise, a returns window, a claim
about who made a piece. In jewelry, provenance claims are held against you. Every template in this
folder is written to avoid this, and `09-open-questions.md` exists so the real answers replace the
blanks rather than a guess filling them.

## What good looks like at 90 days

Not revenue. Revenue at 90 days depends on stock and luck. These are the things that are in your
control, and if they are true the revenue follows:

- Every account is open, branded, secured with 2FA, and owned by the client.
- The grid reads as one shop, not a folder of pictures.
- Twelve weeks of posting with no gap longer than four days.
- Every piece in stock has a SKU, a cost price, a sell price and a photograph taken by you.
- Every enquiry answered inside business hours, same day, without exception.
- You can say, from the sheet and not from memory, what sold last month and what it earned.
