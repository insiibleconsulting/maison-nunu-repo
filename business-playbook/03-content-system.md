# 03 — The content system

The goal is not good posts. It is **posts that keep happening**. A system that survives a bad week
beats a strategy that needs a good one.

---

## Five pillars

Every post belongs to one. If it belongs to none, it is a post about the business's mood and it can
wait.

| # | Pillar | What it does | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The piece** | One item, well lit, price and ref code visible | 40% |
| 2 | **The eye** | Styling, pairing, stacking, what to wear it with | 20% |
| 3 | **The proof** | A customer wearing it, packaging, a handover, a repeat buyer | 20% |
| 4 | **The access** | "Looking for something specific? We can source it" | 10% |
| 5 | **The person** | Who chooses, how, why this piece and not that one | 10% |

**Pillar 3 is the one that converts and the one that gets skipped.** Nobody buys gold from a
stranger. Ask every buyer for permission to post the handover photo, and post it. One real customer
outperforms ten product tiles.

**Pillar 5 is the moat.** A reseller's whole case is "my taste is worth trusting." That case cannot
be made by product photos alone. The client's face and voice, even occasionally, is what separates
this from the hundred other jewelry pages.

---

## Cadence

Start here and do not exceed it for the first three months:

| Surface | Frequency | Realistic time |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed | 3 per week (Mon, Wed, Fri) | Batched in one hour on Monday |
| Instagram stories | Daily, 1 to 3 frames | 5 minutes, unbatched, off the cuff |
| Reels | 2 per week, reuse on TikTok | 30 minutes for both |
| TikTok | 3 per week (the 2 Reels plus 1 native) | Mostly reposting |

**The stories matter more than they look.** They are where the person shows up, where a piece gets
flagged as nearly gone, and where the WhatsApp link sits. Feed posts build the shop. Stories build
the relationship.

One rule: **never go more than four days silent.** A dormant page reads as a closed business, and a
customer deciding whether to send 145 000 FCFA to a WhatsApp number checks.

---

## Batching

One hour on Monday produces the week. Doing it daily is what causes people to stop.

```
  MONDAY, 60 MINUTES
  ├── 0–10   Pick three pieces from the Inventory sheet. Prefer unposted and slow movers
  ├── 10–30  Shoot or pull photographs. 4:5 crop, brand ground
  ├── 30–45  Build tiles in Canva from the three saved templates
  ├── 45–55  Write three captions from templates/caption-library.md
  └── 55–60  Schedule all three in Meta Business Suite for Mon / Wed / Fri
```

Meta Business Suite schedules Instagram and Facebook together, for free. TikTok's own scheduler is
free in the web uploader and handles up to ten days ahead. No paid scheduling tool is needed and
none should be bought.

## What to post when there is nothing new

The hardest week is the one with no new stock. Nine posts that need no new inventory:

1. **A detail macro** of something already posted. New crop, new post.
2. **A styling pairing.** Two pieces already in stock, shown together, one line on why.
3. **A price tile.** Midnight ground, gold Playfair, one piece, its ref code, its price.
4. **A care note.** Pull one from the website's `/guide/` page. It already exists.
5. **Answering a real question** someone asked in WhatsApp. If one person asked, forty wondered.
6. **A sizing explainer.** Ring sizes G to U mean nothing to most buyers. Explain them once.
7. **The gift bands.** Under 75k, under 120k, under 200k. The website already filters by these.
8. **Behind the choosing.** Why this piece was picked and the similar one was not. Pillar 5.
9. **A restock or a last-one note.** Scarcity that is true is the most effective post available.

## What actually works on each platform

**Instagram feed** rewards the still image. Product photography on a consistent ground, shot well,
is still the strongest thing here. Carousels outperform singles because a second swipe is a second
signal. Post a hero, then details, then the piece worn, in one carousel.

**Reels and TikTok** reward movement and the first second. Metal that catches light is genuinely
good short-form material and most jewelry sellers waste it on static slideshows. Formats that work
with a phone and no crew:

- Unboxing a new arrival, unedited, one take
- Hands rotating a piece slowly under window light, 6 seconds, no talking
- "Three ways to wear one bracelet"
- Packing an order for a customer
- A price reveal: show the piece, hold, then the number

**Do not chase trending audio for its own sake.** A trend that does not suit a quiet brand buys you
views from people who will never buy. Use trending audio when it happens to fit.

**Keep video files small.** A significant share of the audience is on metered mobile data. A
90-second video that buffers does not get watched. Under 30 seconds, most of the time.

## Hooks that suit this brand

The first line of a caption and the first second of a video. Quiet, specific, not shouty.

- "This one is not coming back." (only when true)
- "The most asked-about piece this month."
- "What 145 000 FCFA actually buys you."
- "Three ways to wear one bracelet."
- "I almost did not buy this one."
- "The difference between vermeil and plated, in 20 seconds."
- "Looking for something we do not have? Ask."

Every caption ends with the same move: **a question or an instruction that opens WhatsApp.** "Ref
RNG-01. Message us to see it in person."

## Hashtags

Modest returns, but free. Five to eight, mixed by size, in the caption rather than a comment.

```
  Local intent      #douala #yaounde #cameroun #237 #madeincameroon
  Category          #jewelrycameroon #accessoiresdouala #braceletsgold
  Brand             #maisonnunu
```

`#maisonnunu` on every single post from day one, without exception. It is how customer photos get
found later, and it costs nothing to start now and is impossible to backfill.

## The link situation

Instagram allows one link in bio, plus link stickers in stories. TikTok allows one in bio.

**Do not use Linktree.** It adds a hop, it is another account to manage, and it leaks the click.
Point the bio link at `maisonnunu.com` and let the website do the routing. The site already has a
shop with category and price filters, which is a better landing page than a list of buttons.

The exception is the **WhatsApp contact button on the Instagram profile**. Set it up (Edit profile →
Contact options → WhatsApp Business number). It is the single highest-value configuration on the
account, because it turns a profile visit into a conversation with one tap and no link in sight.
