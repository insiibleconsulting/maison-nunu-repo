# Maison Nunu — business playbook

A working manual for taking Maison Nunu from "a website nobody has seen" to a shop that is
findable, credible and easy to buy from.

Written for two readers:

- **The client**, who runs the business from a phone and needs steps, not theory.
- **Insiible Consulting**, who has to set it up, hand it over, and not be needed afterwards.

Read `00-the-strategy.md` first. Everything else is execution, and `CHECKLIST.md` is where you
track it.

---

## The one sentence

**Maison Nunu does not manufacture. It chooses.** Every decision in this folder follows from that,
because a reseller competes on taste, trust and access, not on craft.

Say what that means in practice: nobody buys a gold bracelet from a stranger on the internet. They
buy it from a person whose eye they trust, who answers fast, and who they believe will still be
there next month. Social media's job is not to sell the bracelet. It is to build that belief, then
open a WhatsApp thread.

## Ground rules this folder keeps

These are not style preferences. Each one protects the business from something.

1. **Never claim Maison Nunu made a piece.** "Hand stamped" describes a product and is fine.
   "Our house signet" is an authorship claim and is false. See `CLAUDE.md` invariant 5c. A
   provenance claim about jewelry is something a customer can hold you to.
2. **Never invent a fact to fill a gap.** No guessed payment methods, no invented returns window,
   no supplier names you have not confirmed. An empty section is safe. A plausible wrong one is not.
3. **The client owns every account.** Insiible Consulting is added as a manager and can be removed
   without breaking anything. No brand account is ever registered on a consultant's personal email.
4. **Free or near free.** The domain and, if chosen, one mailbox are the only running costs. Any
   proposal with a monthly fee is flagged as a change of premise, not slipped in.
5. **One intake loop.** A new piece is photographed, given a SKU, logged, published and posted in a
   single sitting, always in that order. See `05-inventory-and-sales.md`.

## The honest blocker

The website is built and works. The catalog inside it is not real. All 75 rows in
`data/products.csv` are `status: placeholder`, the prices are invented, and every photograph is
Unsplash stock licensed for demo use only.

**No amount of social media structure fixes that.** A follower who clicks through to a shop of stock
photos does not come back. The sequence in `08-launch-sequence.md` is built around this: real stock
data and real photographs come first, social accounts open second, and content starts third.

The work in this folder is what makes the shop worth visiting. The catalog is what makes it real.
