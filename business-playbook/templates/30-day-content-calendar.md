# Template — first 30 days of content

A worked first month. Not a rule, a starting shape: it shows the pillar mix, the rhythm and the
batching in practice. After month one, plan a week at a time and never more.

**Cadence:** feed Mon / Wed / Fri, stories daily, two Reels a week reused on TikTok.

**Pillars:** 1 the piece · 2 the eye · 3 the proof · 4 the access · 5 the person.
See `03-content-system.md`.

---

## Week 1 — Arrive as a shop, not as an announcement

The first nine posts go up across three days, prepared in week 3 of `08-launch-sequence.md`. A
profile with nine tiles reads as an established shop. A profile with one tile reads as someone who
might stop next week.

| Day | Feed | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Best-selling piece, hero on beige | 1 | Carousel: hero, detail, worn |
| Mon | Detail macro of the same piece | 1 | Single |
| Mon | Midnight tile: `Fine jewelry & accessories, chosen piece by piece.` | 5 | Single |
| Wed | Second piece, different collection | 1 | Carousel |
| Wed | Styling pair from two collections | 2 | Single |
| Wed | Midnight tile: how to buy. Hours, by appointment, WhatsApp | 4 | Single |
| Fri | Third piece | 1 | Carousel |
| Fri | Who chooses, and why. A face if the client is willing | 5 | Reel |
| Fri | Detail macro | 1 | Single |

**Stories every day this week:** the photography corner, a piece arriving, a poll between two
pieces, the link sticker, the WhatsApp button.

**The action that outperforms all nine posts:** message every existing contact who would want to
know, one at a time, not as a broadcast. See week 4 of the launch sequence.

## Week 2 — Show the range

| Day | Feed | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | New collection, hero | 1 | Carousel |
| Wed | `Three ways to wear one bracelet` | 2 | Reel → TikTok |
| Fri | Price tile: piece, ref, price on midnight | 1 | Single |

**Reels this week:** the three-ways styling cut, and a slow rotation under window light with no
talking.

**Stories:** a sizing explainer, a question box (`Ask me anything about sizing`), one behind the
scenes, and the answer to the best question that comes in.

---

## Week 3 — Start proving it

By now there should be enquiries, and ideally a sale.

| Day | Feed | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | A customer piece, **with written permission**, or a packed order | 3 | Single |
| Wed | `Looking for something we don’t have? Ask.` | 4 | Single |
| Fri | Care note pulled from the site's `/guide/` | 2 | Carousel |

**Reels:** an unboxing of a new arrival, one take, unedited. And answering a real WhatsApp question
on camera.

If there is no customer photo yet, use the packed-order version in `templates/caption-library.md`.
Do not stage a fake one.

---

## Week 4 — Sell deliberately

| Day | Feed | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | The gift bands: under 75k, under 120k, under 200k | 1 | Carousel, one piece per band |
| Wed | `I almost didn’t buy this one` | 5 | Reel |
| Fri | Slow mover, repositioned. New photograph, new angle, honest reason to look again | 1 | Carousel |

**Stories:** a last-one alert if one is genuinely true, a month-end thank you, and a preview of what
is coming in.

**Friday's post is merchandising, not content.** Pull the piece from the Inventory tab's `Days held`
column. The posting schedule is also how dead stock gets moved.

---

## The mix, checked

Across twelve feed posts:

| Pillar | Posts | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — the piece | 6 | 40% ✓ |
| 2 — the eye | 2 | 20% ✓ |
| 3 — the proof | 1 | 20% — **light, fix in month two** |
| 4 — the access | 1 | 10% ✓ |
| 5 — the person | 2 | 10% ✓ |

Proof is under-weighted in month one because there are no customers yet. It is the pillar that
converts, so it should rise as sales start. **Ask for a photo after every single sale.**

---

## Month two onwards

Stop planning months. On Monday, open the Inventory tab and pick three pieces:

1. One that is selling, to keep momentum
2. One that has not moved in 60 days, to shift it
3. One new arrival, or a new angle on something already posted

Then work the batch in `07-operating-rhythm.md`. That is the whole system. It survives a bad week,
which is the only test that matters.
