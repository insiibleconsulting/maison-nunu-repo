# Template — caption library

Fill-in-the-blank captions, one set per content pillar. Replace `{tokens}` and delete the rest.

**Rules that apply to every caption below**

- Sentence case. Typographic apostrophes (’). American spelling: **jewelry**.
- Ref code and price travel together, or neither appears.
- Ends with a way into WhatsApp. Every time.
- **Never claim Maison Nunu made the piece.** "Hand stamped" describes a product and is true
  whoever sells it. "Our house signet" is an authorship claim and is false. `CLAUDE.md` invariant 5c.
- `#maisonnunu` on every post from day one. It is how customer photos get found later and it cannot
  be backfilled.

If the client decides on bilingual captions (question 5 in `09-open-questions.md`), add one French
line at the end, above the hashtags.

```
The Thread band. Sterling silver, 1.2mm, deliberately plain.

#maisonnunu #douala #jewelrycameroon #stackingrings
```

**Short version, for a second post on a busy week**


## Pillar 2 — The eye

```
{Two or three pieces}, worn together.

{Why they work: a shared metal, a contrast in weight, one loud and two quiet.}

Refs {SKU}, {SKU} · {price} FCFA together
Message us and we’ll help you put a set together.

#maisonnunu #styling
```

Alternative openers for this pillar:

- `Three ways to wear one {piece}.`
- `The rule we actually follow: {one specific, arguable opinion}.`
- `If you only buy one thing this month, buy the {piece}. Here’s why.`
- `{Piece A} or {piece B}? They do different jobs.`

An opinion outperforms a description here. The whole pillar exists to show that there is a person
with taste behind the account.

## Pillar 3 — The proof

The highest-converting pillar and the one that gets skipped. **Always ask permission, in writing,
in the chat, before posting a customer.**

```
{Customer first name} took the {name} home last week.

{One line about what she was looking for and why this one.}

Ref {SKU} · similar pieces in stock
Message us if you’re looking for something like it.

#maisonnunu #douala
```

Other forms that need no customer:

```
Packed and ready for its collection today. Ref {SKU}.
```

```
Back in stock, because three people asked. {Name}, ref {SKU}, {price} FCFA.
```

```
Sold. {Name} has found its person. There’s one more like it, ref {SKU}.
```

## Pillar 5 — The person

```
{A short first-person note: why this piece was chosen, what almost got bought
instead, what makes a piece worth carrying.}

{What that means for the customer.}

Ask us anything.

#maisonnunu
```

**Worked example**

```
I almost didn’t buy the raffia hats.

They’re seasonal, they take up space, and I already had three
suppliers telling me they wouldn’t sell. They sold out in eleven days.

I’ve learned to trust the pieces I keep picking up and putting down.

Ask us anything.

#maisonnunu #douala
```

Specific, a little self-critical, and it makes the taste visible. Three or four of these a quarter
is enough, and they will outperform the product posts.

## Reel and TikTok scripts

No script over 30 seconds. Most of the audience is on metered mobile data and a video that buffers
does not get watched.

**The unboxing.** One take, no edit.
> *New in today.* [open] *{Name}, {material}.* [rotate under the light] *{price} FCFA, ref {SKU}.
> One in stock.*

**The rotation.** No talking, trending or quiet audio, six seconds.
Hands turning a piece slowly under window light. Text on screen: name, ref, price. That is all.
This is the cheapest format and often the best performing, because metal in motion is genuinely
good short-form material.

**Three ways.** Fifteen seconds.
Same piece, three quick styling cuts. Text on screen counts `1 / 2 / 3`. End card: ref and price.

**The question.** Twenty seconds.
> *Someone asked me {real question from WhatsApp}.* [answer it properly] *Ask us anything.*

If one person asked, forty wondered. The WhatsApp inbox is the best content brief available.

## Story frames

Lower effort, higher frequency. Daily.

| Frame | Text |
|---|---|
| New in | `New in today. Ref {SKU}.` + link sticker |
| Poll | `{Piece A} or {piece B}?` Two options. People answer polls |
| Question box | `Ask me anything about sizing` |
| Last one | `Last one. Ref {SKU}.` Only when true |
| Behind the scenes | Photographing, packing, unboxing a delivery |
| Repost | A customer’s story, with thanks |

**Never fake scarcity.** In a market this size it is found out, and "last one" stops working
forever the first time it is not true.
